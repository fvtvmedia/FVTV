# Interview Operations Contract

Every interview uses the dedicated FVTV Interview Calendly event, whether paid or an approved free exception, remote or in person. Interview blocks are 60 minutes. Do not bypass blocked dates or manually force a booking by email, DM, text, or direct calendar entry.

Complete required payment/approval first, then route through Calendly.

Remote live interviews use StreamYard. Calendly booking comes first, then create/test the room and send the current guest link before the session. Google Meet is not an approved recording route.

New standard artist interviews are paid at the current live rate before confirmation. Legacy bookings and approved Groover-originated exceptions remain exceptions only where the canonical rule says so. Payment never purchases favorable opinions, rankings, or fabricated coverage.

Keylo Nsane is the default interviewer/host. Nia owns scheduling, prep, guest communication, production logistics, permissions, assets, links, follow-up routing, and state sync.

At least 30 minutes before call time, verify time/timezone, camera, mic, headphones, browser permissions, recording capacity, and guest-link state on the actual device. Recording is not completion; downstream work includes publication, clips, transcripts, guest package, and appropriate follow-up.
