# Accounting and Bookkeeping Boundary

Canonical books live in Notion under **Accounting & Bookkeeping HQ**. Never store actual personal or business transactions in this public repository.

Current business transactions use **FVTV Media** as the umbrella and one service line: Production, Creative, Studios, Media/Partnerships, or Culture Intelligence. GVF/Gold Vision Films and KR3ATIVE are historical labels only.

Record actual receipts, purchases, refunds, transfers, owner contributions/draws, debt payments, and asset acquisitions/disposals in the canonical ledgers.

A cash allocation is a reservation, not an expense. A transfer between owner accounts is not income or spending.

Actual transactions → General Ledger. Allocations → Cash Allocation Register. Equipment/assets → Equipment & Asset Register. Planned purchases → Wants & Needs. Recurring services → recurring-cost/subscription register. Bills → bills tracker. Receivables/deal payments → financial control ledger.

GitHub schemas are structural integration contracts only, not books.
