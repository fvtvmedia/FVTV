# Upwork / Marketplace Revenue Boundary

Live Upwork execution is an authenticated Work-mode lane. Notion remains the task/economics source of truth.

Operating loop: check messages/interviews/offers/contracts; search fresh high-fit jobs; use directly relevant portfolio proof; submit selective job-specific proposals; track Connects, displayed fee, gross bid, expected net, response/interview/offer/contract/loss reason; move replies toward funded contracts; deliver, collect feedback, and measure acquisition economics.

Do not automatically buy extra Connects, boosts, badges, or paid extras. New spend must satisfy the current capital-deployment standard and be justified by observed Connects → interviews → offers → collected cash.

Do not commit client messages, credentials, private proposal data, or live account state here.
