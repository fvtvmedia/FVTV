## Status rules

A sent message is not a reply. A reply is not a booking. A booking is not payment. A platform report is not cleared cash. Close issues only when completion evidence exists or the item is explicitly declined, expired, or suppressed.

Routine social comments are delegated to Echo.me and are intentionally excluded from this index. Revenue-facing social campaigns can be tracked through the editorial issue form with disclosure and approval fields.
