# FVTV Deliverable Control

## Required record

Every external commitment must have one source-of-truth issue or CRM record containing the partner or recipient, workstream, source URL or thread ID, exact promise, owner, date promised, due date, current state, approval gate, assets required, delivery route, and next action.

| State | Meaning | Required next step |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Discovered but not assessed | Capture source and owner |
| Qualified | Fit and route confirmed | Define scope and value |
| Scoped | Deliverables and boundaries written | Check approval and assets |
| Approval needed | Owner, legal, or financial decision required | Pause execution and request approval |
| Ready | Scope, route, assets, and approval are complete | Start work |
| In progress | Work is actively being prepared | Update issue with evidence |
| Review | Draft or asset awaits internal review | Request named reviewer |
| Sent/Published | Delivered externally or published | Capture URL, timestamp, and recipient |
| Awaiting response | Delivery complete; external reply pending | Set a follow-up date |
| Closed | Completed, declined, expired, or suppressed | Record outcome |
| Blocked | Dependency remains after alternatives were tried | Name the dependency and attempts |

## Workstream controls

**Agreements and partnerships** must preserve the original PDF or approved private link, record the terms in plain language, identify renewal and termination dates, and separate “signed,” “received,” “activated,” and “first revenue.” Never change or sign a legal document inside a routine content pull request.

**Affiliates and referrals** require a confirmed program, unique tracking route, commission calculation, payout timing, and reporting owner before anyone is pitched. No revenue is counted until a cleared payout or payment is verified.

**Reviews and Groover** must record the submission, promised review format, deadline, disclosure requirement, status, and final public URL. Editorial review cannot be sold as a favorable outcome. Paid promotion must be a separate, labeled offer.

**Social campaign posts** require the platform, account, asset, caption, disclosure, intended date, link, and approval status. Routine comments and engagement are owned by Echo.me and should not be duplicated here.

**Services** require a payment link or approved invoice route, written scope, asset checklist, delivery window, revision limit, and payment-before-work control.

## Evidence standard

A completed item is not “done” because a draft exists. Done requires evidence: sent-message ID, published URL, uploaded file, payment receipt, partner confirmation, or a clearly recorded decline/suppression outcome.
